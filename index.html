Research

LungCD — clinical decision support for lung disease

My final-year research: a multimodal clinical AI system that reads a chest X-ray, weighs it against the patient's vitals, and tells the clinician how much to trust the result.

Test accuracy

94.8%

Four-class chest X-ray classification

Macro F1

0.8957

Balanced across all four classes, not just the common ones

Trained on

21,165 images

COVID-19, lung opacity, viral pneumonia, normal

Severity module

200 cases

Validated against recorded patient vitals

A classifier that only emits a label is not much use in a clinic. The interesting problem was what the system should do when it is unsure — or when the image says one thing and the patient's vitals say another.

The imaging model is an EfficientNetB0 trained in two transfer-learning phases, with CLAHE preprocessing to recover detail in low-contrast X-rays and class weighting so the rarer categories are not quietly ignored by the loss.

On top of it sits a rule-based severity score that fuses the image prediction with age, SpO₂, fever and cough, and a Clinical Consistency Guard that flags low-confidence predictions and cases where the imaging and clinical signals disagree — rather than handing back a confident-looking answer the clinician has no way to interrogate.

It is deployed as a Streamlit dashboard with Grad-CAM heatmaps showing where the model actually looked, a what-if engine that re-scores severity as you change a vital, and generated case reports.
